# 9. AI assistant and the phone app

## 9.1 AI assistant

The round button in the bottom-right corner of the window opens a chat with an AI model that can build
slopes ("make an 8 m clay slope with the water table 2 m below the crest"), change the open model ("raise
the water table 1 m", "set the clay cohesion to 15") or solve it and explain the result.

Edit > Preferences > AI Assistant turns it on or off and chooses the service: Claude or ChatGPT with an API
key, any OpenAI-compatible service, or your Claude / ChatGPT account through the Claude Code or Codex app.
The assistant sends your message and a summary of the open model to that service.

Help > AI Slope Builder describes one slope in words and builds it.

**Always check the model the AI builds** — every value — before relying on its result.

## 9.2 Phone and tablet app

The Android and iOS apps run the same calculation engine on the phone (no internet needed after
installing). In the app you can build a slope with Quick Slope, open the examples and .slab files made on
the desktop, change material values, the water table, the surcharge and analysis settings, solve any
analysis, and see the results (critical slip surface and F, or contours) and the report. New geometry is
drawn in the desktop program; large models solve faster there.
